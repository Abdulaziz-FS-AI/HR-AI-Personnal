# HR AI SaaS - Intelligent Resume Screening Platform

## Core Concept
AI-powered resume screening and candidate evaluation platform for companies to efficiently process bulk resume uploads (100+ files) with intelligent analysis and scoring.

## Key Components Brainstormed

### 1. Company Profile Setup
**What companies fill before uploading resumes:**
- **Role Information**
  - Job title/position name
  - Department/team
  - Seniority level (entry, mid, senior, executive)
  - Employment type (full-time, contract, remote, hybrid)

- **Role Description**
  - Company background/culture
  - Role responsibilities (bullet points)
  - Day-to-day tasks
  - Team structure/reporting

- **Requirements (Hard/Soft)**
  - Education requirements
  - Years of experience (min/max)
  - Industry experience preference
  - Certifications needed
  - Location requirements

- **Skills Assessment**
  - Technical skills (with importance weights 1-10)
  - Soft skills (communication, leadership, etc.)
  - Nice-to-have vs must-have categorization
  - Tool/software proficiency requirements

### 2. Custom Screening Questions
**Dynamic question builder with weighting:**
- Behavioral questions
- Technical scenario questions  
- Culture fit questions
- Custom company-specific questions
- Importance weighting (1-10 scale)
- Question categories (technical, cultural, experience)

### 3. AI Analysis Output
**What the AI returns for each resume:**
- **Overall Score** (0-100)
- **Category Scores**
  - Technical fit (%)
  - Experience match (%)
  - Education alignment (%)
  - Skills coverage (%)
  - Culture fit potential (%)

- **Key Insights**
  - Top 5 strengths
  - Top 3 concerns/gaps
  - Standout achievements
  - Red flags (if any)

- **Executive Summary** (2-3 sentences)
- **Detailed Analysis** (paragraph format)
- **Interview Recommendations** (questions to ask)

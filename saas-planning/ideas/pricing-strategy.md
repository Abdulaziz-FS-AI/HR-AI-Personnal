# Pay-Per-Resume Pricing Strategy

## Cost Structure Analysis

### AI Processing Costs (Llama 3.1)
- **Per Resume**: $0.001-$0.003
- **Batch of 100**: $0.10-$0.30
- **Monthly Volume (1000 resumes)**: $1-$3

### Infrastructure Costs (Estimated)
- **File Storage**: ~$0.01 per resume
- **Processing Power**: ~$0.02 per resume  
- **API/Platform**: ~$0.01 per resume
- **Total Cost**: ~$0.05 per resume

## Pricing Tiers

- Standard AI analysis
- Basic scoring (0-100)
- Skills matching
- Experience relevance
- PDF export

### 2. Professional Tier: $3.99 per resume ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**  
- Everything in Basic +
- Custom questions analysis

- Team collaboration features

### 3. Enterprise Tier: $6.99 per resume
- Everything in Professional +
- Bias detection reports
- Custom scoring weights

- Priority processing
- Dedicated support

## Volume Discounts
- **10-49 resumes**: 10% discount
- **50-99 resumes**: 20% discount  
- **100+ resumes**: 30% discount
- **500+ resumes**: Custom pricing

## Competitive Positioning
- **Free ATS tools**: Basic keyword matching only
- **Enterprise ATS**: $100-500/month + setup fees
- **Our advantage**: No monthly fees, advanced AI, instant setup
